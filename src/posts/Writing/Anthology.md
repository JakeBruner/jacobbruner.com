### Icicles

Each fright-sicle, stalactite if you will, looms

an eskimo blanket of probability over what befalls us.

surmises on the countless wonts we've always neglected.

### Ephemeral

Cherry blossoms are afraid of their own effervescence.

Bubbling far above tapped-oaks and nameless perennials,

they exclaim for a whole month before reeling back to 

winter's indifference.

A young boy hurls a snowball at the late-blossom—

unaffected as usual.

A mother exclaims back "stop! dont!", as the 

blossum smirks back knowingly

### Violins

Petrichor — the smell of wet-cobble

Metaphor — the smell of dry comparison,

or compassion, was that?

Can kind words assuage and swindle one in the cold?

Probably—

But words can be sharp, painful, cutting, digging

into one's very bones.

From what are we to make of this, indifference?

### Moon's Breath

There was a day, dusk actually, on a beach where I saw the

sea pouring into the stars. Each of the moon's breaths cascaded through

my toes, sinking me deeper and deeper.

The cutting rhythm pantomimed me into a two-step.

Exhillarated, upbeat, CRASH into the blue. Nor did I sail away

for the might. But I lay just beyond the deep end—begging,

pleading to be drawn deeper, whence appeared a black cat.

Timid, but not skittish. Aloof but not unaware.

The pool drained and the cat was yet to be remained. 


### Saxophones

You peer down a latter,

someone beckons you up, "It's just me don't be scared"

Then you remember eached contrived dinner party spent

on the rooftop of his Harlem brownstone.

Either the latter shakes or you shake, despite

the helping hand

You question: do we ever lose a part of ourselves

along the way?

All too quickly he stutters a no.

But wasn't it that stutter apprehended while

climbing the flights to reach you?

you take a long pause to look in his eyes,

and reach a glimmer of terror.

Why would you do this to me?

The latter was read anyways.

### Circles

On our east campus lawn, circles, circles, circles, would

be forced incessantly into my repertoire as flew across the ovular 

roundabout searching for another end? Clearly one knows that circles,

or ovals perhaps, envelop something of a sort. For me, that meant pain.

I rode, carefree, with the occasional punctuated stare from onlookers,

with whom I cherished an air of confusion. Two pedals—now one,

I felt myself blossoming closer to a brick wall, and ending of sort.

But that siren was too beckoning. What other avenues were

there to explore, traveling on a campus in circles, I mean.

### Snakes

Then again, who's life is it?

Probably mine adorned with

trappings, not linings, of silver

slivering my sense of self in

two halves, see?

### Dusk

You wake in a daze. It's been

days since. you last woke, where

am I you ask. The darkness

caws back deafeningly. Are you dead?

You don't *feel* dead? Do you?

On your bedside table, a tornado

with rising water willing your

wit to submerge. How can a

tornadoe happen so suddenly, you wonder

—

You shut your eyes and the

black claws at you. Your

Creator couldn't seem to choose

fate. What a shame!

The windows shatter.

The lights go out.

You try again tomorrow.

### Cold Sweat

Some mornings I wake in a cold sweat.

Drenched in bruises and the stone-cold stares I get.

—

The night jester makes his quotidian visit.

Just as the tide subsides by my side.

—

Awake! An echo yells

never berating, never berating;
